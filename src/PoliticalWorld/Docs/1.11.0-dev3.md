# Political World 1.11.0-dev3

Third development build of the 1.11 cycle.

## Internal party wings and dynamic splinters

- Political parties now accumulate internal faction pressure over time.
- Long-lived one-party states generate faction pressure faster, so a country should no longer be able to sit politically frozen with one organization for centuries.
- At moderate pressure, a party can become **Factional**, representing meaningful internal wings.
- At high pressure, a wing can break away and form a new political party.
- Most breakaway parties either remain in the same ideological family or move toward a politically nearby ideology.
- During severe instability / radicalization, a rare wing can make a much larger ideological break and adopt a politically distant ideology.
- Ideological distance is derived from Political World's existing behavior profiles (market, welfare, centralization, pluralism and militarism) instead of a giant hard-coded pair list.
- When a wing changes ideology, a small deterministic share of low-conviction supporters follows it, giving the new party an actual social base.
- Fresh splinter parties get a short grace period before low-support dissolution so they have time to compete and grow.
- Party caps and existing split cooldowns are still respected.
