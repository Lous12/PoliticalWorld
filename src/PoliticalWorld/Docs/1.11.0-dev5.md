# Political World 1.11.0-dev5

## Electoral alliances

- Compatible parties in competitive systems can now form persistent two-party electoral alliances.
- Alliances prefer ideologically close parties and are especially attractive to opposition parties and near elections.
- Fresh parent/child splinters cannot immediately reunite as an alliance.
- Alliances can dissolve when their members drift too far apart ideologically or in radicalism.
- Elections are now coalition-aware: votes of allied parties are counted together to determine the winning bloc, while the strongest party inside that bloc leads the government.
- The Politics UI shows the winning electoral bloc and its combined vote share.
- Election API event payloads now include coalition id/name/support without changing the public API version.
