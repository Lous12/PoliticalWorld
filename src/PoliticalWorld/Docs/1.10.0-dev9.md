# Political World 1.10.0-dev9

- Fixed vanilla **Whisper of War** being intercepted by Political World's diplomatic crisis/concession system.
- Added a direct forced-war-type check for vanilla `whisper` (and `spite`) wars, with stack detection kept as a fallback.
- Player-forced wars now start immediately, while AI declarations still use Political World's political/diplomatic simulation.
- PoliticalWorldAPI remains 1.18.0.
