# Political World 1.10.0 RC1

Release candidate for Political World 1.10.

## Included in 1.10

- Dynamic political country names with government, ideology and cultural/race naming styles.
- Race political profiles that bias political tendencies without hard-locking outcomes.
- Expanded ideology/framework groundwork for future content and addons.
- Native Settlement Politics tab integrated into the vanilla settlement window.
- Settlement government loyalty and separatist sentiment.
- Active separatism stages: separatist movement, autonomy campaign and secession crisis.
- Player-forced vanilla wars bypass Political World's diplomatic interception.
- Fixed Whisper of War and Spite being intercepted by crisis/concession logic.
- PoliticalWorldAPI 1.18 with settlement separatism access/events and UI/framework additions.

## RC1 test focus

- Long 300–500 year simulations.
- War-heavy / crisis-heavy worlds.
- Regime changes and dynamic country renaming.
- Separatism escalation through secession.
- Existing save compatibility.
- Grand Strategy addon compatibility.

No new large features should be added between RC1 and 1.10.0 unless a release-blocking issue requires it.
