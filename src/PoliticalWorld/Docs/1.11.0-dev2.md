# Political World 1.11.0-dev2

Second development build of the 1.11 cycle.

## Election leadership fix

- Parliamentary and presidential republics now synchronize the vanilla WorldBox ruler with the leader of the party that wins a competitive election.
- If a different party wins, its living party leader is installed as the country's visible ruler.
- Constitutional monarchies are intentionally excluded: elections change the ruling party / head of government, while the monarch remains in place.
- Existing Political World leadership offices are refreshed on the next simulation stage, immediately after elections.
- Uses the same guarded WorldBox ruler-install bridge already used by political crises instead of introducing a second reflection path.

Reported by **@Mauro**.

## Still included from dev1

- Dynamic monarchy rank progression: Barony / County / Duchy / Kingdom / Empire.
- Constitutional Kingdom / Constitutional Empire naming.
- PoliticalWorldAPI 1.19 monarchy-rank access.

Monarchy rank progression suggested by **@Asriel**.
