# Political World 1.11.0-RC1

This build is feature-frozen for 1.11. No new major systems should be added before release.

## Included in 1.11

- Monarchy rank progression from smaller titles up to Kingdom and Empire.
  - Suggested by @Asriel.
- Republican rulers now update when another party wins an election.
  - Reported by @Mauro.
- Dynamic internal party factions and party splits.
- Splinter parties can adopt a nearby ideology and, in severe crises, make a rarer major ideological break.
- Political alliances / electoral blocs between compatible parties.
- Coalition-aware election results: allied parties can combine their electoral strength.
  - Party-system improvements and alliances suggested by @Mars.

## RC testing checklist

- Load an old 1.10 save.
- Test monarchy title progression on small and large monarchies.
- Test parliamentary and presidential republic elections and ruler replacement.
- Let a one-party state run long enough to produce internal faction pressure and a split.
- Check that new splinter parties survive long enough to participate in politics.
- Test ideological changes after party splits.
- Test creation and breakup of political alliances.
- Test an election where an alliance competes with a larger single party.
- Open Political World: Grand Strategy and verify it still detects PoliticalWorldAPI 1.19.
- Check Politics / Settlement Politics UI for missing text or layout problems.
- Save and reload after party splits and alliances have already formed.
