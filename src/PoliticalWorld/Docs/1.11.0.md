# Political World 1.11.0

## Highlights

- Added monarchy rank progression from smaller royal titles up to Kingdom and Empire. Suggested by @Asriel.
- Fixed republican rulers remaining in office after their party lost an election. Reported by @Mauro.
- Added internal party factions and dynamic party splits.
- Splinter parties can move toward nearby ideologies, with rarer major ideological breaks during severe political crises.
- Added political alliances / electoral blocs between compatible parties.
- Allied parties can combine electoral support; the strongest member leads a winning bloc.
- Alliances can later collapse when their members drift too far apart politically.
- Improved long-term party generation so countries are less likely to remain one-party systems for centuries.
- Various balancing, compatibility and UI fixes.
